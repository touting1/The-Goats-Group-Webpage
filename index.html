Our Mission - The Goats

At The Goats, our mission is to work together to create innovative and useful software. As computer science students, we bring different skills and ideas to the team. We value creativity, communication, and teamwork. Our goal is to continue improving our programming skills while building projects we can be proud of.

Our Team Rules

  • Respect everyone's ideas and opinions.
  • Communicate regularly with team members.
  • Complete assigned tasks on time.
  • Help teammates when they need support.
  • Contribute equally to group projects.
  • Be open to feedback and improvements.
